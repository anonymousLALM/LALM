# LALM result provenance

- `source_measurements.csv`: compact source measurements, training identities, relative raw paths and hashes.
- `aggregate_metrics.csv`: aggregate means and sample SDs across completed training checkpoints.
- `aggregate_manifest.json`: hashes and explicit completion status.
- `runs/`: published historical predictions, metrics, configs and final checkpoints.
- `extension_seed_23/`: replacement run outputs and 12 completion receipts, created by the runner.
